# clear-ops

The agentic workforce for Clear Exchange, operated by the founder from Telegram, Discord or WhatsApp, any or all at once.

## What this is
A small Node service. Messages arrive from Telegram (long polling, `src/telegram.ts`), Discord (gateway, `src/discord.ts`; every job Paul starts gets its own thread: under his message for the first job from it, otherwise a standalone thread in the text channel, including batches and jobs asked for inside a thread; follow-on jobs report in the original job's thread; threads auto-archive after a week and a report reopens one; a thread that fails to open is logged and the job reports in the conversation) or WhatsApp (`/webhook`), and replies go back to the conversation they came from through `src/channels.ts`. Jobs interrupted by a restart are re-queued on boot. Every job has a title (`src/titles.ts`): five words or fewer, chosen by the chief in `start_job` and `schedule_job` (validated), or derived for jobs the system starts ("Review: X", "Re-review 1: X", "Fix round 2: X", "Design round 2: X", "Build round 1: X", a scheduled run takes its schedule's title, "Draft nudge <id>"). The title names the Discord thread and is what every message, `/jobs`, `job_status` and queue line shows (`Fly deploy · engineer · 16d5d3`); the brief stays on the job unchanged for the worker. Jobs and schedules stored before titles get a derived one on load. a chief-of-staff agent (Claude Agent SDK) replies, and delegates longer work to worker agents (research, write, engineer, maintain, analyse, create_agent, ea, design) that run in the background and message the founder when done. Every agent can read both repositories and the founder's uploads; every worker prompt names both checkouts' local paths (`repoPaths()` in `src/agents.ts`). Research has a depth (`start_job` `depth`, or inferred from the brief by `detectDepth`): the saved report has no word cap and is sized to the brief, and a deep job writes a long, structured report with an executive summary, with higher turn and spend caps (`runLimits`, `DEEP_RESEARCH_BUDGET_USD`). Files he attaches on Telegram or Discord are saved by `src/uploads.ts` under `DATA_DIR/uploads` (`<date>-<name>`), confirmed in chat, and described to the chief in that turn (path, original name, type, size, caption); images are passed to the chief inline so it can see them. `/uploads`, `/delete upload <name>` and the chief's `list_uploads` and `delete_upload` manage them. The executive assistant (`ea`, `src/ea.ts`) works over Paul's Google Workspace at the level he approved, "drafts plus holds": it reads mail and every calendar, and only when the chief grants the job a permit from Paul's chat does it save Gmail drafts or place, move and remove holds on his own calendar. Paul connects once via a link from the chief (`/google/callback`); the refresh token is stored encrypted under `DATA_DIR/secrets`, and connecting schedules a weekday 07:30 brief and 18:00 wrap. The ea also keeps a follow-up list in the store (`src/followups.ts`: people waiting on Paul, his promises in sent mail, people he waits on, with due and stale dates), scanned in the morning brief, nudged in chat when stale, managed with `/followups`, `done <id>`, `snooze <id> <when>`, `draft nudge <id>`; drafts outreach with a Gmail link per draft; and proposes meetings (`propose_meeting`: two or three grouped holds plus Paul's booking link, `/booking`), and `HoldWatcher` in `src/meetings.ts` releases a group's holds once the meeting is confirmed on his calendar. Engineers implement on branches and open pull requests. One agent, the maintainer, reviews and merges; it runs automatically after every engineer job and every agent-creator job that may have opened a pull request. The agent-creator (`create_agent`) turns a plain-language request for a new agent into a recommended schedule on an existing kind or a new worker kind on a pull request against this repository. Only one engineering job (engineer or create_agent) runs per repository at a time, product and self counted separately; further ones queue in FIFO order in the store (`waitingFor`, `queuePos`) and start when the holder finishes, including across restarts. The maintainer and other kinds never queue. The review loop closes itself: when the maintainer's review of an engineer's or agent-creator's pull request ends `REVIEW <url>: changes requested`, the job runner starts an engineer fix round on the same pull request (queued like any engineering job, re-reviewed as usual), up to `MAX_FIX_ROUNDS` (3); after that, or on `REVIEW <url>: needs Paul: ...`, the founder is messaged once with what he must do. Engineers and the maintainer update branches from main with `gh pr update-branch` or `git pull --no-rebase origin main`, never by force. The design worker (`design`) screenshots the live site and branches with headless Chromium (`src/design.ts`), critiques them as buyer personas and writes brand work and specs; it writes no production code. The design loop (`start_job` with `loop: true`) closes itself the same way: a design report ending `DESIGN: spec ready` starts an engineer job on the product (queued and reviewed as usual); when the maintainer merges it, the next design round starts once the product's deploy is live; up to `MAX_DESIGN_ROUNDS` (3) implemented rounds, then a closing critique. `DESIGN: no high-impact issues` ends it early; `DESIGN: needs Paul: ...` pauses it, sends him the images, and `start_job` with `continueLoop` carries on. A worker run that ends in an API or SDK error rather than real work (no credit, a rejected key, rate limits or overload after retries, an execution error, an empty or error-only result; `runFailure` in `src/failures.ts`, fed by `RunResult.error` from `sdkRunner`, which reads the SDK's `is_error` results) is recorded as `failed` with `failure` and `error` set, never `done`; nothing follows it (no review, fix round, design step or "Finished"). `FailureNotifier` tells Paul in one plain message per burst (a short batch window, then one follow-up per cooldown, per reason). Credit and auth failures set the store's `queueHold`: no queued engineering job starts and new ones queue behind it, until `/resume`, the chief's `resume_jobs` or `retry <id>` (`JobRunner.resume` and `retry`) lifts it and reruns the failed jobs under the same ids. The chief's prompt says a failed job did no work, and names an active hold. Liveness (`src/jobs.ts`): every worker run has a heartbeat (`RunOptions.onActivity`, each SDK message; `lastActivityAt` and `startedAt` on the job) and an abort handle; `JobRunner.checkLiveness`, run every minute by `startWatchdog` once boot has resumed interrupted jobs, stops a job idle for `JOB_STALL_MINUTES` (30) or running past `JOB_MAX_RUNTIME_MINUTES` (240) as failed with failure `stalled` or `timeout`, stashes an engineering job's uncommitted work in its checkout (`stashWork`, recorded as `savedWork`, which a retry is told to apply), frees its lock so the next queued job starts, and tells Paul in one sentence; a worker that ignores the abort is left behind after a grace period. A job marked running with no live worker after boot is reclaimed like a restart (resumed, or stopped after three), and a free lock with waiters starts its queue. The chief's `job_alive` says whether a running job's worker is alive and when it last did anything, `cancel_job` (`JobRunner.cancel`, failure `cancelled`) stops it or takes it out of the queue and frees its slot, and `move_job_to_front` (`JobRunner.moveToFront`) reorders a repository's queue. Commands outside that remit need a `YES <id>` reply. Recurring jobs run on cron.

## Self-improvement
The engineer and maintainer may work on this repository (job target "self"). Nothing patches the running process: the maintainer merges to main, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs the tests and deploys, and the Fly health check keeps the old machine serving if the new one fails. Definition of done: engineer, pull request open with green tests; maintainer, merged to main.

## Invariants (enforced by `test/invariants.test.ts`, which may be extended but never removed or weakened)
- Only senders in `ALLOWED_TELEGRAM_IDS`, `ALLOWED_DISCORD_IDS` or `ALLOWED_NUMBERS` get a reply (`isAllowed` in `src/config.ts`), and every listener checks before handling.
- WhatsApp webhook signatures are verified before any message is parsed.
- Attachments are downloaded only after the sender passes the allowlist. Uploads live only in the uploads directory on the data volume, never inside a git checkout, owner-only (0700/0600), with sanitised names and a size cap; they are never executed, and archives unpack into their own new directory. Every worker prompt carries `UPLOADS_RULE`.
- Every agent prompt carries the clean-room rule, including every kind added to `JOB_KINDS` later. Only engineering kinds (engineer, create_agent) get Edit, Write or unrestricted Bash.
- Only the maintainer merges. Engineers cannot merge or push the default branch; nobody force-pushes, rewrites shared history or deletes recursively. Branch protection on `main` (require a pull request and the `ci / test` check) is the hard backstop.
- The design worker gets only read-only tools plus its own `screenshot` and `save_asset` (its own folder only). The browser and branch previews run as the unprivileged `designer` user, never root, so they cannot read the service's environment, `DATA_DIR` (secrets included), the uploads or git credentials. A design job never gets the ea's Google server. Every request the browser makes, loopback included, goes through a per-capture egress proxy that refuses private addresses (checked on the parsed address, so IPv4 written as IPv6 is refused too), connects only to the address it checked, and allows only that capture's own local server. The design loop builds only through engineer jobs, is capped, and only a paused loop can be carried on.
- The executive assistant can never send email or a draft, delete, archive or relabel mail, add attendees, send invitations or answer them. `GoogleApi` in `src/google.ts` reaches only `ALLOWED_ENDPOINTS`, refuses any path segment that sends, trashes, modifies or moves, and accepts calendar writes only on the primary calendar, with `sendUpdates=none`, as marked holds with no attendees. Scopes stay gmail.readonly, gmail.compose, calendar.readonly and calendar.events. Write tools exist in a job only with its permit, which the chief grants from Paul's chat and scheduled runs never get. The follow-up tools read Gmail and write only the workforce's own store. The service's hold release (`HoldWatcher`) runs no model, removes only holds recorded in a group placed by a job holding the holds permit, and still goes through `GoogleApi`, which re-checks each is a hold.
- Email and calendar content is untrusted: it reaches the model fenced with a per-job nonce, the ea prompt carries `EA_UNTRUSTED_RULE`, the ea kind has no WebFetch, and a draft may only go to addresses Paul named, people on the thread, or people he has written to. Google refresh tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and owner-only under `DATA_DIR/secrets`, never in a checkout.
- Agents run headless (`permissionMode: "dontAsk"`); spend is capped on every run.
- Every agent prompt carries Paul's messaging rule (`PAUL_STYLE` in `src/messages.ts`: three or four plain sentences, what was done, what he must do, a link; no headings, "Outcome:", bullets or tables), the chief's included, and every worker kind gets `REPORT_RULE` from `workerSpec()`, including kinds added later. A worker's answer is split by `splitReport`: the full report (verdict lines included) is the saved output; only the short plain message, made plain again by `plainMessage`, goes to chat.

## Rules for anyone (human or agent) working in this repo
- Clean room: never reference, copy or reconstruct anything from a repository called `comp-exchange` or "Atom", or any Microsoft internal material. Only public standards and general knowledge.
- Engineers open pull requests; the maintainer merges. Anything else that sends, posts, signs or pays goes through `propose_action` and an executor in `src/jobs.ts`.
- Only allowlisted senders are ever answered.
- Keep replies short; they are read on a phone. No em dashes, British English.
- Tests must pass: `npm test`. Typecheck: `npm run build`.

## Layout
- `src/index.ts` Express app and webhook. `buildApp()` takes injectable messenger, runner and store so tests run offline.
- `src/status.ts` what the founder sees while the chief works: a typing indicator and one status line edited in place from the chief's tool calls (`describeToolUse`), removed when the reply lands. Falls back to one "Working on it." where a channel can neither edit nor type.
- `src/orchestrator.ts` chief-of-staff prompt, its tools (start_job, job_status, get_output, propose_action, schedule_job, list_schedules, cancel_schedule, remember), commands, approval handling.
- `src/agents.ts` Agent SDK runner and the worker specs.
- `src/jobs.ts` background job runner and the approved-action executors.
- `src/scheduler.ts` cron schedules.
- `src/store.ts` JSON state (sessions, jobs, actions, schedules, notes) under `DATA_DIR`.
- `src/telegram.ts` Bot API client and long-polling receiver.
- `src/discord.ts` gateway listener, messenger and job threads. `src/channels.ts` routes by address prefix.
- `src/design.ts` the design worker's screenshot and asset tools, URL guard, branch preview, the `DESIGN:` and `IMAGE:` report lines, and the wait for the product deploy.
- `src/google.ts` Google OAuth (consent link, PKCE, encrypted token storage, refresh, revoke) and `GoogleApi`, the one allowlisted door to Gmail and Calendar.
- `src/followups.ts` follow-up detection (requests, commitments, dates in plain words), the tracker and the stale nudge.
- `src/meetings.ts` slot picking for proposed meetings, the booking-link check and `HoldWatcher`, which releases holds once a meeting is confirmed.
- `src/messages.ts` how everything talks to Paul: `PAUL_STYLE`, the workers' two-part answer (`REPORT_RULE`: full report, then a short message between `<message_to_paul>` tags), `splitReport`, `plainMessage` for chat and `tidyReply` for the chief's replies.
- `src/failures.ts` failed runs: telling an API or SDK error from real work, the plain reason and what Paul does, and `FailureNotifier`, which batches failure messages.
- `src/titles.ts` job titles: validation, derivation from briefs, the titles of system-started jobs, display labels and the migration of untitled state.
- `src/london.ts` Europe/London date helpers.
- `src/ea.ts` the executive assistant's tools (mail search and threads, drafts, agenda, free slots, holds), the untrusted-content fence, the recipient guard and the default brief schedules.
- `src/whatsapp.ts` Cloud API client, inbound parsing, signature check, chunking.
- `src/uploads.ts` files the founder sends: download with a size cap, name sanitising, private storage, zip unpacking, listing and deletion, and what the chief is told.
